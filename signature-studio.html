How to Install Your VELOLOG Email Signature

Follow the quick steps below for your preferred email client:

  1. Click the green "Copy Signature" button above.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings (gear icon) > See all settings.
  3. Scroll down to the Signature section and click Create new (or edit your existing signature).
  4. Paste directly into the text box using Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac).
  5. Set your default signature for "For new emails use" and "On reply/forward use".
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
  1. Click "Copy Signature" above.
  2. Open Outlook and click File > Options > Mail > Signatures...
  3. Click New and give your signature a name (e.g. VELOLOG Official).
  4. In the Edit signature box, paste the signature using Ctrl+V.
  5. Under Choose default signature, select your account and choose the new signature for New messages and Replies.
  6. Click OK to save.
  1. Click "Copy Signature" above.
  2. In Outlook Web / Office 365, click the Settings gear in the top right.
  3. Go to Mail > Compose and reply.
  4. Under Email signature, click + New signature and paste using Ctrl+V.
  5. Select default options for new messages and replies, then click Save.
  1. Click "Copy Signature" above.
  2. Open Apple Mail, navigate to Mail > Settings... > Signatures.
  3. Select your account and click the + button to add a signature.
  4. Uncheck "Always match my default message font".
  5. Paste your signature into the editor window using Cmd+V.
