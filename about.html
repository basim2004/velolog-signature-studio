Dedicated 80,000 SQM Terminal in Dammam

We operate our own terminal in Dammam, strategically located near the seaport area in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Spanning 80,000 square metres, our terminal is fully fenced, securely guarded, and designed to meet diverse customer requirements.

Equipped with modern material handling equipment and managed by experienced professionals, we ensure the efficient loading, unloading, storage, and transfer of cargo. Our focus is on delivering high-quality, reliable terminal services that minimize delays and maximize operational efficiency for our clients.
